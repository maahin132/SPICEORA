# Owner Confirmation Checklist

Unknown business and launch information remains **Pending**. This document is not evidence of ownership, certification, availability, policy approval, or legal readiness.

| Topic | Status | Owner confirmation required |
|---|---|---|
| Legal company identity | Pending | Registered legal name, entity type, jurisdiction, tax identity, and official publication name |
| Registered address | Pending | Verified registered and trading addresses |
| Official business contacts | Pending | Verified phone, email, postal address, and business sender identity |
| Ownership and governance | Pending | Authorized owners, approvers, and administrative contacts |
| Farm locations | Pending | Actual farm names, locations, ownership/authorization, boundaries, and evidence |
| Real product catalogue | Pending | Which verified products are eligible for publication |
| Product specifications | Pending | Variety, origin, grade, moisture, processing, packaging, size, shelf life, and lab evidence |
| Quality standards | Pending | Actual quality controls, inspection process, tolerances, and retained evidence |
| Certifications | Pending | Certified body, certificate scope, expiration, jurisdiction, and public display approval |
| Product photography | Pending | Rights-cleared, accurate photography and approved alt text |
| Export documentation | Pending | Required commercial invoice, packing list, certificates, inspection, and destination documents |
| Pricing policies | Pending | Currency, validity, taxes, freight terms, quotation policy, and public or private visibility |
| Minimum order quantities | Pending | Verified MOQ and packaging-based order constraints |
| Shipping | Pending | Incoterms, origin/destination scope, carriers, lead times, and risk allocation |
| Payments | Pending | Approved methods, deposits, milestones, currencies, refunds, chargebacks, and fraud controls |
| Returns and disputes | Pending | Approved return eligibility, rejection process, claims, evidence, and timelines |
| Product availability | Pending | Confirmed stock or production information with a refresh/update process |
| Privacy policy | Pending | Jurisdiction, controller identity, lawful basis, retention, rights, processors, and approved publication |
| Terms and conditions | Pending | Website, enquiry, quotation, sale, limitation-of-liability, and governing-law terms |
| Cookie policy | Pending | Required cookies, optional analytics, consent model, and jurisdiction review |
| Customer communications | Pending | Approved email/SMS copy, response times, notification consent, and delivery provider |
| Production email infrastructure | Pending | Production domain, transactional provider, sender identity, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, quotas, and monitoring |
| Hosting and domain | Pending | Approved domain, hosting provider, HTTPS certificate, DNS ownership, and environment separation |
| Production database | Pending | Approved database host, identity separation, credentials, TLS, retention, backup, and recovery requirements |
| Backup strategy | Pending | Backup schedule, encryption, retention, access, restore owner, and verification frequency |
| Disaster recovery | Pending | Recovery objectives, runbooks, contacts, failover or restoration scope, and exercise dates |
| Monitoring and logging | Pending | Log owner, PII redaction, retention, alerting, incident channel, and security response |
| Production deployment approval | Pending | Security review, performance review, content approval, data migration approval, and go-live authorization |
| Data retention and deletion | Pending | Enquiry, account, OTP, session, audit, inventory, and operational record schedules |
| Staff access roles | Pending | Named administrators, permission groups, MFA, joiner/mover/leaver process, and periodic review |
| Enquiry workflow | Pending | Triage ownership, response targets, status meaning, notes policy, and escalation |
| Quotation workflow | Pending | Whether multi-product quotations, line items, validity, and approvals are required |
| Buyer/account features | Pending | Required customer capabilities beyond registration and profile access |
| Downstream export integrations | Pending | CRM, accounting, ERP, warehouse, carrier, and document-system requirements |

Status values:

- **Confirmed:** backed by approved owner information and retained implementation or documentation evidence.
- **Pending:** no owner confirmation or verified evidence has been recorded.
- **Not applicable:** a specifically justified owner decision determined the item is outside scope.

Do not change a Pending item to Confirmed solely because an implementation checkbox is complete. Confirmed legal, certification, product, logistics, payment, privacy, and policy content requires evidence outside source code.
